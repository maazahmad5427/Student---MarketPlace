# Sequence Diagram Notes

The editable Draw.io files in this folder cover the two expanded Assignment 4 trading use cases:
1. Request and accept an item reservation (FR08–FR10, FR13, FR17, NFR08).
2. Confirm a completed sale after the offline exchange (FR11–FR13, FR17, NFR08).

The diagrams model the UI, trading service, persistence and actors. The original SRS states that acceptance and completion must keep reservation/listing states consistent and that physical payment/exchange happen outside the application.
